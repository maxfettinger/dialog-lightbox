Minimal

The least that works: links to the large files, a <dialog> with a viewport, one call, and only the library's own CSS.

There are no buttons and no styles of its own. Escape, the back button, a tap beside the photo or a drag down close it. Arrow keys and swipes move between photos, and a click zooms. The last link is a video, which plays muted; a tap pauses it.

Sensible

What most sites want: square thumbnails, a dark box with a counter, buttons and a caption, and video controls on videos.

The links carry the native size and a srcset, so the box opens with a zoom out of the thumbnail at once and loads a file that fits the screen. The panorama zooms to its full width.

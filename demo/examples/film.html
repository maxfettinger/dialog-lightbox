Film strip

The look of maxfettinger.com: photos on a strip of 35mm film, opened in a dark viewer like a phone's story view.

The frames are cropped by CSS, and the viewer still opens with a zoom out of each frame to the whole photo. On videos the play, sound and fullscreen buttons and the progress bar sit inside the video, through an overlay template that moves with it.

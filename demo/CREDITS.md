# Demo media

For now every photo and clip in `media/` is generated with ffmpeg's `gradients` source and resized and
re-encoded for the demo. They carry no copyright. They have the shapes a lightbox needs to be tested on:
landscape and portrait photos, a very wide panorama for zooming, a portrait clip with sound and one without.

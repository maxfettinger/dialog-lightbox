This is the look of [maxfettinger.com](https://maxfettinger.com), the site this library was built for. The frames are cropped by CSS, and the viewer still opens with a zoom out of each frame to the whole photo. On videos the play, sound and fullscreen buttons and the progress bar sit inside the video through an overlay template, so they move, swipe and shrink with it. The captions name each photo's author and license.
